Methodology

How we count

We measure the ecosystem, not individual organisations: projects entering and leaving the directory each quarter.

Growth formulas

Net growth

projects at end of quarter − projects at start

Quarterly growth rate

(new projects − removed projects) ÷ projects at start × 100

Example for Q4 2026: (3 − 12) ÷ 188 × 100 = −4.8%.

What counts as new, active or removed

New: a project listed in this quarter's infographic that was not in the last one. We record its country, category, date added and how we found it.

Removed: a project that is no longer listed because it shut down, went inactive, stopped operating or can no longer be verified.

Renamed or moved: counted once. A move between countries lowers one count and raises the other.

Africa Wide listings

Pan-African projects are counted in the totals but not shown on the map, since they don't belong to a single country. They grew every quarter of 2026: 29, 31, 35, 37.

Known differences from posted figures

Q3 2026 was posted as 189 listings, 23 countries and 13 new. Our count finds 188, 25 and 14. Category totals for Q3 follow the posted 189.

Q4 2026 includes one repeated listing (Bitcoin In The Wild). Counting it, the growth rate is −4.3%.

Limits of the data

If a project stops operating without telling us, the directory may not notice right away. We combine Live Directory data with regular manual research rather than relying on self-reported updates alone.
